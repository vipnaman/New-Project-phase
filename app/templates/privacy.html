Privacy

Privacy policy

RAKTA handles account and donation-related information to operate donor discovery, matching, and request coordination. This notice is a product summary and must be reviewed by qualified counsel for the regions where the service operates.

Information we use

Account details include your name, email, and optional phone number. Donor profiles may include blood group, location, availability, and donation dates. Blood requests may include patient name, blood group, hospital, location, urgency, and details you choose to provide. Avoid entering sensitive medical information that is not needed to coordinate a donation.

Who can see your information

Public donor search shows approved donor names, blood group, approximate location, availability, and verification state; it does not display email or phone. Private donor profiles are not listed. Phone details are shared with the other participant only after a contact request is accepted. Administrators can access information needed to review accounts, listings, and safety reports.

Notifications and account removal

In-app notifications are used for matching and request updates. You can turn email notifications off in account settings. SMS notifications are not currently available. You can request account removal from Settings; this disables sign-in and anonymizes profile information. Some records may be retained or anonymized for operational, safety, or legal reasons, subject to the final retention policy for the regions served.

Before launch

Do not use RAKTA as a substitute for emergency medical services or clinical advice. The service must not be promoted publicly until the operator publishes contact details, applicable legal terms, a retention schedule, and a process for privacy requests and data incidents.
